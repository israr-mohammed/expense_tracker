Privacy Policy

How Expense Tracker handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Expense Tracker is a personal expense-tracking application. If you create an account, we collect the account details you provide, such as your email address and password. If you add expenses, we collect the information you enter for each expense, such as amount, category, date, and description.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your account and expense information solely to provide the core functionality of the app — letting you log in, record expenses, and view your spending. We do not use your information for advertising.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored in order to make it available to you when you use the app. This project is a personal / demo application and does not implement enterprise-grade security infrastructure, so it should not be used to store sensitive financial or personal information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your account or expense information with third parties.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Expense Tracker does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. Any cookies used are limited to what is needed for the site to function, such as keeping you signed in.

6. User Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and expense data by contacting us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Expense Tracker after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the Expense Tracker team through the app.
